News

Announcement Oct 15

Homecoming Spirit Week Schedule Released

Five days of themes, one big pep rally. See what to wear.

Spirit Week kicks off Monday, Nov 2 with Pajama Day and ends Friday with the pep rally and game. Each grade is competing for the spirit trophy, with points awarded for participation, hallway decorations and the lunchtime games.

All dress-up days must follow the regular dress code. Questions can go to the ASB advisor in Room 150.

Clubs Oct 14

Robotics Team Advances to Regionals

The Eagles Robotics squad earned a top-three finish.

After a strong weekend of qualifying matches, the robotics team secured a spot at the regional championship. The team thanks its mentors and the parents who helped with travel and build days.

New members are welcome. Meetings are Tuesdays and Thursdays after school in Room 122.

Academics Oct 13

Library Antelope Valley College Hours

Quiet study space now open until 5:00 PM on weekdays.

Starting this week, the library will stay open until 5:00 PM Monday through Thursday. Tutoring is available on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, and printing is free for students.

Sports Oct 12

Cross Country Wins League Meet

Both boys and girls teams took first place on Saturday.

The cross country program swept the league meet, with four runners finishing in the top ten. The team now prepares for the divisional race in two weeks.

Announcement Oct 9

Picture Retake Day Reminder

Missed picture day? Retakes are next Thursday in the library.

Students who were absent or want a retake can come to the library during their PE or elective period with a pass. Bring your original proof if you want a replacement.
